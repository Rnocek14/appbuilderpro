# Build to be bought — the exit strategy for the drift

*Fifth panel of the interrogation series, convened on the founder's declared goal: "I want to
build to be bought… what should the platform be, really think hard into others and how this is
similar to." Four lenses reasoned backward from acquirers — the M&A record of
interaction/mechanic acquisitions, the acquirer map, a platform-shape bake-off, and
solo-founder dealcraft — then a referee forced the platform choice. Prior context:
`dot-field-strategy.md` (the wedge, the velocity thesis, the verdict, the capture plan).*

---

## 1. What the M&A record actually pays for

Nobody acquires a gesture. The record, deal by deal:

- **FingerWorks → Apple (2005, likely single-digit $M).** The multitouch patents that became
  the iPhone — sold for a rounding error, because one bidder, no cohort, no distribution.
  *The cautionary anchor for "demo + patent" state.*
- **atebits/Tweetie → Twitter (2010, low single-digit $M).** Pull-to-refresh's inventor,
  acquired for an engineer's signing bonus. The mechanic detached from the app instantly;
  only the app was priced. *Why the provisional + naming trail exist in our capture plan.*
- **Mailbox → Dropbox (2013, ~$100M, five weeks post-launch, $0 revenue).** The asset was a
  1.3M waitlist — a *publicly countable velocity instrument*. Manufactured scarcity at peak
  hype, window measured in weeks.
- **Acompli → Microsoft (~$200M) vs Sunrise → Microsoft (~$100M).** The 2× spread is the
  "becomes the acquirer's product" premium (Acompli = Outlook mobile, alive today) vs the
  "design + team" price (Sunrise, killed). *Be the thing they rename, not the thing they strip.*
- **tbh → Facebook (2017, ~$30M+, nine weeks post-launch).** A mechanic plus a cohort
  Facebook couldn't reach (US teens). *Cohort identity beats cohort size — the most relevant
  comp for a founder whose content engine can summon a niche cohort on demand.*
- **Musical.ly → ByteDance (~$1B).** Requires a network. Ceiling, not plan.
- **Dark Sky → Apple / THE YES → Pinterest / Workflow → Apple.** Datasets and taste teams are
  legible acquisition categories; without traction they price as acquihires.
- **Immersion Corp.** Haptic IP as a standalone business = a decade of litigation-licensing.
  File the provisional as optionality and diligence armor; do not plan on Immersion economics.
- **Halide / Things (non-exits).** The indie cash-flow posture whose real function in dealcraft
  is *the ability to refuse a lowball*.

**The pricing ladder for this founder:** demo+IP only: $0.5–10M (don't sell here) · one
retained niche cohort + accruing dataset: $10–40M · engine-manufactured viral cohort + dataset:
$30–150M, time-boxed to the hype peak · threat-scale: $500M+, requires a network and funding.

**The three price-depressing mistakes in the record:** one bidder and a quiet process; selling
before a countable asset exists; letting the mechanic detach from the property (Brichter). All
three have standing counters in the plan.

## 2. The platform ruling

**One sentence: build the drift *runtime* — a bounded, finishable daily world over any
judging-dense corpus — shipped as Scout first (unchanged) and as a podcast Audio Drift world
second, so that by mid-2027 one company owns the primitive, the patent, and a two-domain
micro-verdict dataset an acquirer must rename and ship.**

- **Scout stays exactly as staked** (TestFlight-100 ritual, kill criteria, dogfood loop) — but
  architected from day one as *world #1 of a corpus-agnostic runtime*: the daily-world spine
  (bounded corpus, fade-on-read, trails, journeys) with a swappable ingestion adapter. The
  Acompli lesson at near-zero present cost.
- **The exit surface is podcast Audio Drift.** The paradigm's one honest weakness is scent —
  cards must be *read*, re-importing the throughput loss. A 1.5s audio hook is pre-attentive:
  audio is the only corpus where the 40ms-rejection thesis gets *stronger* under scrutiny, and
  podcast discovery is broken precisely because sampling a show costs 20 minutes — rejection
  speed is the cure for the category's disease. The **Podz → Spotify (~$45–50M, 2021)** comp is
  directly on point: Spotify bought ML-cut audio hooks feeding a discovery feed; the drift is
  the interaction layer Podz never had, plus the steer-away signal Spotify cannot measure.
  Critically, the build has **no catalog-API risk**: podcast RSS enclosures are open; hooks are
  cut server-side (exactly Podz's legal path). Spotify's own preview/recommendation endpoints
  died for new apps in Nov 2024 — music via API is likely dead, so music becomes the
  *acquirer's expansion slide*, not the build risk.
- **Bidder count sets price:** audio has 4–6 credible rooms (Spotify, Apple, Amazon Music,
  SoundCloud, YouTube Music) where shopping/moodboard has ~1 (Pinterest) and dating has zero at
  solo scale (they buy user bases, not primitives). Dating and general-feed shapes are dead as
  builds; Match stays on the slate anyway (below). Vibe-worlds shopping remains the *option*
  if audio retention fails — the dataset carries over.
- **The trigger:** Scout passes its retention gate (~M6) → Audio Drift world begins. What
  carries over: the spine, the recommender, the cross-domain dataset, the patent claims.

**Amendment (applications research, 2026-09-01 — see `dot-field-applications.md`):** world #2
changes from podcasts to **Sample Drift** (loops/one-shots for producers). The audio thesis
strengthens rather than weakens — a musical transient is judged in <400ms where speech needs a
~30s clip — and the corpus is legally free (Freesound, 72% CC0) where podcasts carried licensing
posture. Buyer slate reorders accordingly: Splice (bought Spitfire ~$50M, Apr 2025), Loopcloud,
Native Instruments, Output first; Match stays the second room. Honest bands shrink to $1–5M
for the exit case but with four named, recently active buyers instead of one.

## 3. The buyer slate

1. **Spotify.** Wound: discovery is the moat narrative; podcast discovery is broken; Podz gave
   them clips but no interaction layer and no negative signal. Proof that moves them: **the
   verdicts-per-session chart** — three drift sessions predicting subscribe/complete better
   than N sessions of tap data — plus a working podcast world. Bands: demo+patent $5–15M;
   retention+chart $30–80M.
2. **Match Group / Bumble — always the second room.** Wound: swipe fatigue is a stated
   earnings-call risk; they litigated the swipe, so they *believe gestures have cash value*.
   Proof: not a dating product — any retained judging-dense cohort plus the filed signal-method
   patent. Bands: $20–50M; viral moment $50–150M.
3. **Pinterest.** Wound: a finishable world cannibalizes browse-time ad economics — they
   structurally cannot build it, making buy-or-shelve rational; Cosmos is bleeding their taste
   class. Proof: an engine-made viral "drift your moodboard" demand moment. Bands: $15–60M.
4. **Snap.** Wound: needs an invented primitive to justify existing beside TikTok; their
   demographic lives inside the engine's reach. Proof: the tbh path. Bands: $15–50M — lowest
   ceiling, most reachable, and a live third bidder is worth more than its own price.

**Apple is press strategy, not plan** (engineer the launch for Daring Fireball/MacStories
taste — one Gruber post outprices TechCrunch). **Meta/ByteDance are the clock**: the clone
threat, made legible by the patent + dated naming trail, is every other buyer's deadline.

## 4. The 12-month calendar (build + dealcraft interleaved)

- **M0–1:** File the provisional (signal-method claims: hysteresis selection + dwell/steer-away
  telemetry — the sensor, not the dots). Split entities: drift IP + apps ≠ content engine.
  Write the AI-provenance declaration now (all code authored in founder-owned repos; standard
  2026 diligence item — a one-pager ends the conversation). Weekend-spike the podcast
  hook-cutter to de-risk world #2.
- **M2–3:** Scout TestFlight-100. Publish the dated naming write-up. Quiet $100–300K on SAFEs
  from acquirer-alumni angels (ex-Apple HI, ex-Tinder product) — intro networks without a
  preference stack. A priced VC round poisons a sub-$30M exit. **No corp-dev contact** — an
  early meeting is free due diligence for their clone team.
- **M4–5:** Public launch; all 50 accounts fire; demo video engineered for the Apple press
  bubble (haptics, restraint, "finishable").
- **M6 — read the curves against triggers:** ≥100K installs still accelerating → *velocity
  path*, take meetings within 30 days (the Mailbox/tbh window is 8–16 weeks and does not
  reopen) · D30 ≥20–25% with verdicts/session holding → *retention path*, keep building, start
  Audio Drift, talk at M9–12 · neither → Halide posture or the kill criteria.
- **M7–9:** Audio Drift TestFlight. Produce THE chart. "I judged 200 podcasts in 90 seconds"
  content wave — the drift *makes sound*; the engine makes it famous. Data room live:
  retention dashboard, rejection-latency distribution, dataset + consent language, IP chain,
  canonical-origin press trail.
- **M10–12:** If triggered: parallel conversations (Spotify + Match minimum, Snap warm),
  M&A lawyer retained (banker not worth it under ~$50M). LOI discipline: never one meeting,
  never name a number first, no exclusivity >30 days, **no earnouts tied to the acquirer's
  roadmap** (milestones you don't control are donations — the Wunderlist lesson).

## 5. Honest odds (5-year, solo)

- **No exit / cash-flow only: ~55%.** Halide posture, $200–500K/yr possible, patent retained
  and separately salable.
- **Acquihire $1–5M: ~18%.** Demo + patent, no cohort. *Standing rule: refuse — the fallback
  is why you can.*
- **Product acquisition $10–50M: ~20%.** D30 curve + the verdicts-per-session chart +
  two-domain dataset + ≥2 rooms. **The realistic target; every calendar item services it.**
- **Fear-scale $100M+: ~4%.** Requires the engine to manufacture a genuine hockey stick and
  the clone-fear window timed right. Plan to *recognize* it, not depend on it.

## 6. Three decisions no panel can make

1. **Will you spend 2–4 years inside the acquirer?** FingerWorks' founder did 15 at Apple;
   Workflow's did 5+. A mechanic acquisition IS an employment contract. If no — steer toward
   IP/asset-sale structures now and accept roughly half the headline. Deciding at LOI time is
   deciding under duress.
2. **Does the content engine go in the deal?** Ruling from the record: separate entity,
   excluded from a Spotify/Match deal — it's the CAC machine for your next three companies,
   and bundling invites ToS diligence on 50 accounts. Only you know if you're building a
   career of launches or one exit.
3. **Velocity-sale or retention-sale temperament?** If M6 hockey-sticks, the tbh move is to
   sell *before* retention truth arrives — and you must be emotionally capable of selling at
   peak fear. If you're constitutionally a builder, pre-commit to the retention path now; the
   classic failure is holding a velocity asset until fear decays into a spreadsheet.

*The one-line reconciliation: Scout is kept verbatim; the drift is the moat, not the SKU;
podcasts are the exit surface; Match is the second room; the patent is the clock you put on
everyone else.*
