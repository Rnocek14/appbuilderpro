P14 · the drift — staged world, simulated steering

What to feel for

  • Scrub as before. Then push into an edge with intent and hold — the world flows under your thumb, ticking as it streams. Release: it stops dead. You push, it never pushes.
  • Brush an edge casually mid-scrub: the world must not move. The conveyor arms only on deliberate outward pressure.
  • Dwell on what you like, then keep drifting that way — unseen territory ahead materializes thicker with it, and the label top-left names the pull.
  • The glowing weather under the dots is real information: each nebula is a topic current. Read the land before you touch it.
  • Back-scrub your own path: the exact same dots, same positions, your fades intact. The walked world never rewrites.
  • Your route is drawn on the land. The Trail tab is the journey as an object — tap any stop to glide back to it.
Honest label: the "algorithm" is a seeded simulation — 2,420 cells, twelve staged currents, a dwell-weighted sampler for territory you haven't walked yet. What's real is the steering loop, the trail, and the feel. A real engine would put embeddings under the same thumb — and keeping THAT geography stable at scale is the open, unproven risk.
