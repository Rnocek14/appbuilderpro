What to feel for

  • Park your thumb between two dots. The selection must hold — no flicker, no buzz.
  • Scrub edge-to-edge in half a second: an even ratchet, the halo within one dot of your finger, the card readable the instant you stop.
  • One tap per crossing, never two for the same dot, never a hum.
  • Rest on a dot: the card deepens. Lift: it opens. Lift mid-flick: nothing opens.
  • Watch the field fade as you go — the day is finishable, and you can see how much is left.
  • After a minute, reach for a dot you remember. That reach is the whole idea.
